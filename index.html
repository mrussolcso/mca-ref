MCA § 45-5-502. Sexual assault.
Offense Degree Misd / Felony
Recommended Bond $1,000 / Court-Set
Must Appear? YES
Statutory Fine Range $0 - $50,000
  1. (1)
    A person who knowingly subjects another person to any sexual contact without consent commits the offense of sexual assault.
  2. (2)
    Except as provided in subsections (3) and (4):
  3. (a)
    on a first conviction, fined up to $1,000, county jail up to 1 year, or both;
  4. (b)
    on a second conviction, fined up to $5,000, imprisonment up to 5 years, or both;
  5. (c)
    on a third or subsequent conviction, fined up to $10,000, imprisonment up to 10 years, or both.
Source: Montana Supreme Court Recommended Bond Schedule & MCA § 45-5-502
